# Driftwood Bay
A little island community built through gathering, making, and sharing.

## Language
**Island day**: A session-paced period of exploration ending when the player rests at home.
_Avoid_: Real-world day, daily reset

**Shells**: The island's currency, earned by helping neighbors and selling catches.
_Avoid_: Bells, money

**Neighbor request**: A small favor with an item requirement, friendship, and a shell reward.
_Avoid_: Mission, order

**Decoration**: A crafted furnishing placed on open ground, which can be picked up again.
_Avoid_: Structure, building

**Welcome bench**: The first decoration that creates a meeting place and opens the island picnic.
_Avoid_: Tutorial object

**Field guide**: The collection of fish species encountered on the island.
_Avoid_: Museum, encyclopedia
